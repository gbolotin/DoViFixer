# DoViFixer: YouTube Shorts script (47s)

Structure: Hook (0–3s) → Problem → Solution → CTA. About 47 seconds, matching the Kokoro voiceover in `clips/voice/`.

## HOOK (0–3s)

🎙️ *"Your Dolby Vision movie? It's NOT playing in Dolby Vision."*

📱 On screen: **"Dolby Vision? 🤨 Not really."** Fast zoom on a TV showing a flat, dull picture.

## PROBLEM (3–18s)

🎙️ *"Most 4K Blu-ray movies use Dolby Vision Profile 7. But Apple TV, NVIDIA Shield and Fire TV can't play it properly! You get a flat HDR10 picture, or part of the video just gets dropped. You paid for the best picture… and you're not seeing it!"*

📱 Quick cuts: names of the three boxes as text, then a big red ❌ over each. Text pops: **"Profile 7 = 😩"**

## SOLUTION (18–41s)

🎙️ *"Meet DoViFixer, a FREE Windows app! Add your movies and hit Scan. It checks every file and tells you which ones are safe to convert. Then one click turns them into Profile 8.1, real Dolby Vision on your streaming box! The 4K picture stays bit for bit. Audio, subtitles, chapters: untouched. And your original file? Never changed. Want to go back? Keep a tiny backup and restore it anytime!"*

📱 Screen recording: Media page → Scan → risk verdicts → Convert → progress cards. Text pops: **"FREE" · "Original stays safe ✅" · "Profile 7 → 8.1 ⚡"**. End on a before/after split screen with the vivid picture.

## CTA (41–47s)

🎙️ *"You deserve to watch in perfect quality. Link in the description. Go fix your movie library!"*

📱 Text: **"You deserve perfect quality. 🍿"** with the DoViFixer logo and a "Link in bio ⬇️" arrow.

## Production notes

- **Label the before/after shots as simulated.** The docs say the comparison pictures are simulated and results depend on the player app and firmware. Add a small "Simulated" caption so the video doesn't overpromise.
- **Keep "safe to convert" in the voiceover.** By default the app skips Complex FEL movies and only converts Simple FEL if you turn that on. The line also keeps "one click" honest.
- **Pacing:** use one visual cut every 1–2 seconds, burn in captions (most Shorts are watched muted), and add a "whoosh" or bass hit when the picture switches to Dolby Vision at about 39s.
